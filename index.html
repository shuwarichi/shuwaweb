TERMS & CONDITIONS

1. GENERAL & PAYMENT TERMS

  • Payment Method: Payments are accepted via PayPal only.
  • Upfront Payment: 50% of the full payment is required upfront before work starts. Remaining 50% paid after approved sketch.
  • Right to Refuse: I reserve the right to decline any commission or adoptable claim at my discretion.

2. REFUND POLICY

  • Artist Cancellation: If I cancel your commission before or during work, you will receive a 100% full refund.
  • Client Cancellation: Full refund if requested before work starts. 50% refund during the sketch phase.
  • No Refunds: No refunds will be given once lineart/coloring has started or after un-watermarked adoptable files are delivered.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & USAGE

  • Personal Use: Commissions and adoptables are for personal use (profile pics, banners, VTuber profiles, personal prints).
  • Commercial Rights: Merch, branding, or monetization requires a commercial fee of €10.
  • Credit: You must credit me (Shuwa Lichi) when posting or displaying the art/design.
  • AI / NFT Prohibition: Strictly NO uploading my artwork/designs into AI generators, datasets, or minting as NFTs.

4. ADOPTABLE SPECIFIC RULES

  • Holds: Holds are allowed for up to 48 hours maximum. If payment is not made, the design goes back up for sale.
  • Reselling: You may freely resell the character to your liking.
  • Trading & Gifting: You may gift or trade the character to your liking.
  • Redesigning: You may change the character design to your liking.
